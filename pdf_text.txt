1. System Overview 
The Web-Based School Visitor Log Management System is a Django-based application designed to replace 
or support the traditional paper visitor logbook used at school entrances. It focuses on non-student visitors 
such as parents or guardians, alumni, delivery personnel, contractors, government personnel, invited 
guests, and other individuals who enter the school for an official or personal purpose. 
Authorized school personnel will record a visitor’s information, purpose, destination, time-in, and time-out. 
The system will also show who is currently inside the school, maintain searchable visitor history, and provide 
basic visitor statistics. The goal is to make visitor records easier to organize, retrieve, and monitor while 
keeping the project realistic for the course timeline.

10. Scope 
The system will focus on digitally recording and monitoring non-student visitors entering and leaving the 
school premises. It will include authentication for authorized users, visitor registration, visit and time-in/time-
out recording, active visitor monitoring, visitor history, search and filtering, basic dashboard statistics and 
reports, and management of authorized users and school offices/destinations. The application will be web-
based and designed to remain usable on common desktop and mobile screen sizes. 
11. Limitations 
The system will not replace student attendance, employee attendance, enrollment, grading, or physical 
access-control systems. Visitors will not be required to create personal accounts. The initial version will not 
include biometric identification, facial recognition, RFID/NFC gates, automated government-ID verification, 
or automated background checking. The accuracy of visitor records will depend on the information provided 
by visitors and entered by authorized personnel. 
12. Example Scenario 
A parent named Maria Santos arrives at the school to request records from the Registrar’s Office. The 
security personnel logs in, searches whether Maria already has a visitor profile, and creates one if 
necessary. The staff selects Parent/Guardian as the visitor type, records “Request school records” as the 
purpose, selects Registrar’s Office as the destination, and records the time-in. Maria then appears on the 
Active Visitors page with the status Inside. When she leaves, the staff clicks the Time Out action. The 
system records her departure time, changes the visit status to Completed, and keeps the record in Visitor 
History for later searching and reporting. 
13. Development Approach 
1. Set up Python, Django, MySQL, Git, and the Django project in Visual Studio Code. 
2. Configure Django to use the local MySQL database instead of the default SQLite database. 
3. Create Django models for visitors, offices, and visits; use Django authentication for authorized users. 
4. Register appropriate models in Django Admin for administrative management.

15. Final Concept Summary 
In summary, the project is a manageable Django + MySQL school visitor-management application centered 
on a simple real-world workflow: register the visitor, record the reason and destination, time the visitor in, 
monitor who is currently inside, time the visitor out, and preserve the visit in searchable history. Django 
Admin will support administrative data management, while the actual day-to-day visitor workflow will use a 
custom responsive Bootstrap interface. The first version deliberately avoids advanced hardware and 
identity-verification features so the group can focus on a complete, working, and well-designed core system.
